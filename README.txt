Why only two legal pages?
The separate Cookie Policy and Disclaimer pages were removed to avoid unnecessary legal pages for the current site. Relevant cookie/third-party asset notes are part of the Privacy Policy; portfolio and external-link notes are part of Website Terms. This assumes the site continues not to use analytics or advertising trackers and that the enquiry form continues to open the visitor's email application instead of submitting data to a backend.

Seamless hero video
The hero background uses two overlapping copies of the same video and crossfades near the end of playback to hide the loop seam. The video is paused when the hero is not visible or the browser tab is hidden, and reduced-motion preferences are respected. If the browser cannot play the second copy, the site falls back to the native video loop.

Freelance identity
Frame Motive is presented as the name used by an independent freelancer, not as a registered company or startup. The policy pages are practical baseline website terms, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Review them against your actual practices and applicable law before launch.

Before launch
1. Replace https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com in canonical URLs, structured data, robots.txt, and sitemap.xml with the final domain.
2. Confirm the contact email and phone number. Current site uses hello.framemotive@gmail.com and +91 94919 78618.
3. If you add analytics, advertising, non-essential cookies, payment/booking tools, or a server-side enquiry form, update the Privacy Policy and add any notices or consent controls that are required.
4. Test the site in current desktop/mobile browsers and verify the remote hero video can be fetched by the production host.

External dependencies
Some fonts, imagery, and the hero video load from external providers and require an internet connection.
